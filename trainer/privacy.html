Flyx Trainer Privacy Policy

Last updated 18 October 2026

Controller: Julian Kratzer, julian_kratzer@web.de

Flyx Trainer works completely offline. There is no account, no ads, no tracking, no analytics and no purchases. We do not receive any data from you.

  • Study progress: progress, mistakes list, streak and exam results are stored only on your iPhone. They are never sent anywhere. You can delete them at any time under More → “Reset progress”; deleting the app removes them too.
  • No network access: questions and explanations are built into the app. The app downloads nothing and sends nothing.
  • Rating prompt: after you pass a mock exam, iOS may show Apple's rating prompt once per version. Apple processes whether and how you rate; we receive no information about you.
  • Links: if you tap a link (App Store, this website), the App Store or Safari opens. Their privacy rules apply there.
  • Feedback by email: only if you write to us do we receive your address and message. We use both only to reply.

Note: Flyx Trainer is a study aid and not an official offer of any aviation authority. You take the official exam with your national aviation authority (in Germany: the Luftfahrt-Bundesamt, LBA).

You can exercise your rights (access, erasure, complaint to a supervisory authority) at any time by email. Since we store no data about you, this usually only concerns emails.
