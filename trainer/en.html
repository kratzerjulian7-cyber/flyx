Flyx Trainer – Drone Theory

EU A1/A3 & A2 exam practice · Last updated 18 October 2026

Flyx Trainer helps you study for the EU A1/A3 proof of online training and the EU A2 certificate of competency: 236 questions in plain language, explanations with sources, mock exams in the format of the EU regulation (A1/A3: 40 questions, A2: 30 questions, pass mark 75 %) and a mistakes list that brings wrong answers back at growing intervals.

  • Offline: no account, no ads, no tracking, no subscription. Your progress stays on your iPhone.
  • English & German: the app follows your iPhone's language. EU rules are kept general; rules that only apply in Germany are marked “Germany only”.

Important: Flyx Trainer is a study aid and not an official offer of any aviation authority. You take the official exam with your national aviation authority (in Germany: the LBA). A good result in the app is no guarantee for the real exam; the rules in force are what count.
