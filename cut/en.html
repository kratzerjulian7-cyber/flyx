Flyx Cut – automatic video edits

Version 5.0 · Last updated 18 October 2026

Flyx Cut turns your drone, FPV and action cam clips into a finished video in minutes: the app finds the best moments, cuts them to the beat and exports for Reels, TikTok, Shorts and YouTube – fully offline on your iPhone.

  • Automatic edit: modes for Freestyle, Cinematic, Crash and Bike/Ski; your subject stays in frame.
  • Camera detection: Flyx Cut recognises DJI, GoPro and FPV footage from the video data and picks matching settings. You can change the camera at any time.
  • Supported: DJI · GoPro · FPV · iPhone, MP4 and MOV files.
  • Gyro stabilisation, level horizon, looks and export up to 4K.
  • Examples: a demo project in the app shows you how it works before you add your own clips.

What does Flyx Cut cost?

Flyx Cut is free: no subscription, no in-app purchases, no ads, no account, no tracking. Every exported video includes a small “Flyx Cut” mark (optionally with your @handle) and a short end card “Edited with Flyx Cut”. In vertical videos the mark sits inside the safe zones of Reels and TikTok.
