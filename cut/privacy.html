Privacy Flyx Cut

Last updated 13 October 2026

Controller: Julian Kratzer, julian_kratzer@web.de

Flyx Cut has no account, no ads, no tracking and no analytics. Your videos are analysed and edited entirely on your iPhone and are never uploaded. We do not receive any data from you.

  • Photos and videos: Flyx Cut only reads the clips you select and only saves finished videos to your photo library when you tap "Save to Photos". Flight data from DJI subtitles (height, speed, distance, GPS) is used on the device for the overlay only.
  • Projects, ratings and feedback notes: stay on your device and, if iCloud Drive is on, in your own iCloud Drive (folder "Flyx Cut"). Only you and Apple can access them.
  • Sharing: Only when you tap Share or Instagram is the video handed to the app you choose. Its privacy rules apply there.
  • Feedback by email: Only if you write to us do we receive your address and message. We use both only to reply.
  • Subscription and Lifetime purchase (Flyx Cut Pro): Purchase, payment and cancellation are handled entirely by Apple. We receive no payment details and no personal information; the app only checks on the device whether Pro is active.

You can exercise your rights (access, deletion, complaint to a supervisory authority) at any time by email. As we store no data about you, this usually only concerns emails.
