Flyx Fix Privacy Policy

Last updated 12 October 2026 · applies from Flyx Fix 1.0

Controller: Julian Kratzer, julian_kratzer@web.de

Summary: The tools store their data on your iPhone. Jobs, quotes and invoices run through Apple's iCloud (CloudKit), with no separate Flyx Fix account and no password. Quotes, contact details and project history are end-to-end encrypted. No ads, no tracking, no payment data.

Roles

The client is the customer, the contractor is the business. Contracts are made directly between them. Flyx Fix only provides the technical platform and is not a party to the contract.

Tools on your device

Room measure, calculators, meter readings, utility bill checker, defect and handover report and home inventory store their data only on your iPhone. It only leaves your device when you create a job request from a tool and send the prefilled details.

iCloud instead of an account

Flyx Fix uses your iCloud account via Apple's CloudKit. There is no separate Flyx Fix account and no password. Data is stored in the app's public and private iCloud containers at Apple.

Jobs and quotes

  • Before hiring, businesses only see the postal code area and the content of your job (description, photos and documents you add). Your contact details stay hidden.
  • After hiring, the business you hired receives your contact details to carry out the job.
  • End-to-end encrypted are quotes, contact details and the project history (e.g. questions, change orders, sign-off, invoice).

Remove personal details from photos and documents before adding them to a job.

Price ranges

Regional price ranges are only formed from at least 5 real quotes and are evaluated anonymously, without identifying customers or individual businesses.

Businesses and invoices

Businesses create a business profile that the app uses to fill in their quotes. Invoices are issued by the business in its own name, and the business is independently responsible for them. The invoice check in the app only checks the mandatory details formally (Section 14 German VAT Act) and is not tax advice.

Reports

Content and users can be reported. Reports go to the Flyx Fix administration and are reviewed there.

No payments, no ads, no tracking

Nothing is paid in the app, there are no fees and no payment data. Flyx Fix shows no ads and does not track you.

Permissions

  • Camera: only when you take photos/videos or scan a room with LiDAR.
  • Microphone: only when you start a feature that needs it, e.g. noise measurement in the defect report.
  • Photos: only the images you select.

Document text recognition runs on-device (Apple Vision).

Deletion

You can delete your data in the app. Data a hired business has already received also remains with that business.

Service providers

Apple (iCloud/CloudKit). When you download the app, Apple also processes data under its own privacy policy; we only receive anonymous statistics if you agreed to share them with developers.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection, and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. Just email julian_kratzer@web.de.

Changes

If the app's features change, this policy will be updated first.
