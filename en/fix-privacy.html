Flyx Fix Privacy Policy

Last updated 12 October 2026 · applies from Flyx Fix 1.0

Controller: Julian Kratzer, julian_kratzer@web.de

Summary: The tools work without an account and their data stays on your iPhone. For job requests, quotes, orders and invoices you sign in with Apple; this data is stored in Apple's iCloud (CloudKit). No ads, no tracking, no payment data.

Roles

The client is the customer, the contractor is the business. Contracts are made directly between them. Flyx Fix only provides the technical platform and is not a party to the contract.

Tools on your device

Room measure, calculators and My Home (rooms and measurements, photos, defects, inventory, meter readings, imported bills) are stored only on your iPhone. They only leave your device when you create a job request from them and send the prefilled details.

Sign in with Apple

When you sign in we receive an Apple user identifier and, if you share them, your name and email address (possibly Apple's relay address). There is no password.

Data in iCloud (CloudKit)

For the platform, Flyx Fix stores this data in Apple's public and private iCloud containers:

  • Job requests with the description, photos and documents you add
  • Quotes, questions and messages
  • Business profiles
  • Change orders, sign-offs with photos, and invoices
  • Reviews

Remove personal details from photos and documents before adding them to a request.

Businesses and invoices

Businesses create a business profile that the app uses to fill in their quotes. Invoices are issued by the business in its own name; the business is independently responsible for its invoices and for the data it needs to carry out the job.

Reports

Content and users can be reported. Reports go to the Flyx Fix administration and are reviewed there.

No payments, no ads, no tracking

Nothing is paid in the app, there are no fees and no payment data. Flyx Fix shows no ads and does not track you.

Permissions

  • Camera: only when you take photos/videos or scan a room with LiDAR.
  • Microphone: only when you start a feature that needs it, e.g. noise measurement in the defect report.
  • Photos: only the images you select.

Document text recognition runs on-device (Apple Vision).

Deleting your account

You can delete your account in the app at any time. Data you have already sent to a business you hired also remains with that business.

Service providers

Apple (iCloud/CloudKit, Sign in with Apple). When you download the app, Apple also processes data under its own privacy policy; we only receive anonymous statistics if you agreed to share them with developers.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection, and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. Just email julian_kratzer@web.de.

Changes

If the app's features change, this policy will be updated first.
