Flyx Fix Privacy Policy

Last updated 12 October 2026

Controller: Julian Kratzer, julian_kratzer@web.de

Summary: Flyx Fix does not collect personal data. No account, no ads, no tracking, and no data is sent to a server.

Data on your device

Everything you enter or capture (rooms and measurements, photos, defects, inventory, meter readings, imported quotes and bills) is stored only on your iPhone. We have no access to it. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Permissions

  • Camera: only when you take photos/videos or scan a room with LiDAR.
  • Microphone: only for noise measurement in the defect report when you start it; nothing is recorded or sent.
  • Photos: only those you select.

Document text recognition runs on-device (Apple Vision).

Sharing

When you export via “Share”, you choose the recipient; that app's terms apply.

App Store

Apple processes data under its own privacy policy when you download the app. We only receive anonymous statistics if you agreed to share them with developers.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection, and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. Since we store no data about you, you can remove all data in the app or by deleting it. Questions to julian_kratzer@web.de.

Changes

If later versions add Sign in with Apple or iCloud sync, this policy will be updated first.
