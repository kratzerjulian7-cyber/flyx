Features

Everything for flying day in one app

From the garage to the last landed pack: Flyx Map stays with you all day.

Home

Fly today?

Home shows right away whether it works out: weather, airspace and gear at a glance, plus today's best flying time as a curve.

  • Best flying time with wind, gusts, rain and sunset
  • Alerts, e.g. when a pack has been sitting full too long
  • Start a flight or open the map with one tap
Map

What applies here?

Tap the map to see whether you can fly there, whether it's restricted or needs authorization. “Why?” shows rules, heights and legal basis.

  • Official geo zones for Germany, Switzerland, France and the USA
  • Search by place, spot or coordinates
  • Linked straight to the official check
Spots

Your spot as a flight briefing

Every spot bundles what you want to know before you drive out: airspace, weather, best flying time, photos and driving time.

  • Tabs for overview, airspace, weather and photos
  • Route with Apple Maps
  • “Fly here” plans the flight at the spot
Plan a flight

A checklist instead of a gut feeling

Pick spot, drone and battery, set the flight time. Flyx Map checks airspace, weather, daylight and battery and shows what's missing.

  • Timer with voice callouts: goggles on, phone in your pocket
  • Flights go into your log automatically
  • Batteries count their cycles
Garage

Your fleet, always ready

All quads and camera drones at a glance, with photo, class, cells and flight status.

  • Drones, batteries and parts in one garage
  • Pictures per drone type or your own photo
  • Camera-drone mode for DJI and others
Build & maintenance

Every part, every flight

For each drone: installed parts, flights, time in use and the next service. Part broken? See what was installed and whether a spare is in stock.

  • Build with motors, stack, VTX, receiver and camera
  • Maintenance by flights, e.g. check screws
  • Missing parts go on the shopping list
Batteries

LiPos under control

Battery health in percent, charge cycles and a warning when a full pack sits too long.

  • Filters for in use, storage and tired
  • Charged and storage with one tap
  • A reminder before the pack suffers
Recognize products

Type, scan, done

Type a name, scan a barcode or photograph the box. Flyx Map fills in manufacturer and specs from its catalog of more than 600 parts.

  • Knows brands like BetaFPV, RadioMaster, CNHL, Caddx and Holybro
  • Barcodes and photos are analysed on your iPhone only
  • Goes straight to your batteries, parts or drones

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What does Flyx Map cost?

Nothing. Every feature is free, with no in-app purchases and no ads.

Do I need an account?

No. Open the app and go. Crew only needs a display name.

Is the map a clearance to fly?

No. Flyx Map shows the official geo zones clearly but does not replace a check. Use “Check officially” before every flight.

Which countries have geo zones?

Germany, Switzerland, France and the USA. Garage, batteries, spots and flight log work everywhere.

Where is my data?

On your iPhone and, if you turn it on, as a backup in your own iCloud Drive. Details are in the privacy policy.

Does it work with DJI and other camera drones?

Yes. Switch to camera-drone mode under More; your FPV data stays saved.

Is Flyx Map on Android?

Right now it's iPhone only.
