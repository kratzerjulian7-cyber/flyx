FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How much does Flyx Fix cost?

Nothing. Nothing is paid in the app and there are no fees. You pay the business directly for the job.

Do I need an account?

No. Flyx Fix uses your iCloud, there is no separate account and no password.

Who is my contract partner?

The business you hire. Flyx Fix only provides the platform and is not a party to the contract.

Which types of quotes are there?

Cost estimate, non-binding quote and binding quote valid until a set date.

Where is my data stored?

Tool data on your iPhone. Jobs, quotes and invoices in Apple's iCloud (CloudKit); quotes, contact details and project history are end-to-end encrypted. See the privacy policy for details.

What do businesses see before I hire them?

Only the postal code area and the content of your job. Your contact details stay hidden until you hire a business.

Do the calculators show prices?

No. They calculate quantities, duration and output. Regional price ranges only appear once there are 5 real, anonymous quotes.

Can I delete my data?

Yes, in the app.

Do I need an iPhone with LiDAR?

For the room scan, yes. All other tools work on any iPhone.

Which languages are available?

The app is currently available in German only.

Is Flyx Fix available for Android?

Currently iPhone only.
