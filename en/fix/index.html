FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How much does Flyx Fix cost?

Nothing. There are no in-app purchases and no ads.

Do I need an account?

No. Open the app and get started.

Do I need an iPhone with LiDAR?

Only for the room scan. Entering measurements by hand works on any iPhone with iOS 17 or later.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your iPhone. Flyx Fix sends nothing to a server. See the privacy policy for details.

Does Flyx Fix replace professional advice?

No. Calculators and checkers give estimates and do not replace professional or legal advice.

Which languages are available?

The app is currently available in German only.

Is Flyx Fix available for Android?

Currently iPhone only.
